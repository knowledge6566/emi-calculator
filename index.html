Questions

Are these calculators free to use?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no paywall, and no limit on how many times you calculate.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. Choose from the full list of world currencies. The currency and the region's number format are set separately, so you can, for example, show dollars with Indian digit grouping.

Are the results exact?

They are estimates based on the rate and tenure you enter. Banks and funds can add fees, insurance, taxes, or rate changes, so check the final figures with your lender or provider.
